Rajasthan is a popular tourist destination in India known for its forts, palaces, and vibrant culture. Some of the most well-known places to visit include Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, and Ranthambore.

Major Cities and Attractions:

  1. Jaipur (The Pink City):

    2. The capital of Rajasthan, known for its forts like Amer Fort and Hawa Mahal, as well as the City Palace and Jantar Mantar.

  2. Udaipur (The City of Lakes):
    3. Famous for its beautiful lakes, including Lake Pichola and Fateh Sagar Lake, and the City Palace complex.

  3. Jodhpur (The Blue City):
    4. Home to the impressive Mehrangarh Fort, Umaid Bhawan Palace, and Jaswant Thada.

  4. Jaisalmer (The Golden City):
    5. Known for its Jaisalmer Fort, havelis (mansions), and Thar Desert experiences like camel safaris.
