RAJASTHANI BOLI

Rajasthani refers to a group of Western Indo-Aryan languages primarily spoken in Rajasthan, India, and parts of Pakistan and neighboring Indian states. While Hindi is the official language, Rajasthani languages like Marwari, Mewari, and Dhundari are distinct regional languages that form the linguistic identity of the state. These languages have historically faced marginalization due to the dominance of Hindi for administrative and literary purposes, which has prevented their constitutional recognition and widespread institutional use.
